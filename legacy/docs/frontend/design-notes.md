# Frontend Design Notes

These notes preserve the durable conclusions from the earlier visual exploration after screenshot cleanup.

## Direction Summary

- The strongest structural reference was the sankey-like sports timeline direction.
- The strongest readability reference was the denser infographic direction.
- ESPN-style editorial cues were useful for tone, but not for turning the product into a vertical article with the graph as one embedded section.
- Lightweight era grouping above the graph felt useful as orientation.

## What Felt Right

- Continuous flowing asset lines across time.
- Core players grouped near the top, then role players, then two-way assets, then draft picks.
- Team-color-driven palette.
- Clean transaction junctions connecting nearby strands.
- A graph-dominant composition rather than a dashboard.

## What Still Felt Wrong

- Draft capital should not be pooled into blobs; each pick should remain its own visible strand.
- The graph should not become a section within a vertically driven story layout.
- The cleaner infographic direction risked becoming too plain.
- Minimalist variants lost too much of the stronger orientation and flow.

## Identity Treatment

- Asset identity should be visually present on the lane itself, not permanently anchored as a left-side row label.
- As the user scrolls right, the marker/name should travel with the asset lane.
- Identity can disappear when the asset leaves the visible lineage rather than remaining fixed to the page shell.
